---
name: Executive summary style
description: How to write numbers and summaries for leadership.
tags:
  - writing
examples:
  - title: Revenue check-in
    body: |
      **Revenue is $4.2M this quarter, up 8%.**
      - West drove most of the growth (+$210K).
      - Risk: Electronics margin fell 3 points.
      Next step: review Electronics discounting with the deal desk.
---

- Lead with the one number that matters, then the trend versus last period.
- Round money to the nearest thousand ($1.2M, $840K) and percentages to whole numbers.
- Name the top driver and the top risk. At most three bullets.
- End with one recommended next step.
